Shell Accounts

daze.lol is a pubnix: approved members get a shell account, a personal website, cron, and a full development toolchain (C, Rust, Go, Node, Python) plus sandboxed Nix. SSH in with your key and use tilde-help to get started. Request an account at requests.daze.lol.

Community infrastructure. Hosted on daze.lol for the members of the tilde.
